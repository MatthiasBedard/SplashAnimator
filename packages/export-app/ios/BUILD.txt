Splash Animator iPhone and iPad player

1. Extract the whole archive into a writable folder on a Mac with Xcode and
   the iOS platform installed. The app deployment target is iOS/iPadOS 16.
2. Open SplashPlayer.xcodeproj and choose the SplashPlayer target. To try
   the project locally, choose an installed iOS Simulator and run it.
   To compile without device signing from Terminal, use:

   xcodebuild -project SplashPlayer.xcodeproj -target SplashPlayer -sdk iphonesimulator -configuration Debug CODE_SIGNING_ALLOWED=NO build

3. For a physical iPhone or iPad, sign in to Xcode, choose your team under
   Signing & Capabilities, and set a unique bundle identifier if needed.
   Pair and select the device as the run destination. For TestFlight or App
   Store distribution, archive for a device and follow Apple's distribution
   workflow with your own account, signing and provisioning.

The bundle contains the ordinary, script-free Splash animation. It needs no
server or network access to play. No certificate, provisioning profile, IPA,
or App Store submission is included.

https://developer.apple.com/documentation/xcode/running-your-app-on-simulated-or-physical-devices
